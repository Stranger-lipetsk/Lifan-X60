# Isolated Dalvik method harness for original and modified Home (Android 4.4.2 x86)
NOT a firmware, not a head-unit test. Home was dynamically loaded, not installed or started normally.
Real Home.onCreate, HomeService, hardware, audio, boot, preferences and Hibernate were not run.
Original and patched Home resources and methods were loaded in separate classloaders, using stock framework jars; generic Android services are not vendor services.
The harness is signed with the public AOSP emulator platform key and requests INTERACT_ACROSS_USERS_FULL solely to exercise startActivityAsUser; this key is NOT the device platform key.
Amaze v3.5.3 was network-blocked; the panel and Amaze were launched by the original gotoEleadApplication method on the patched in-memory object.
